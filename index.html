How each concept was made

The same four steps, repeated ten times — with a different UX problem at the centre each time.

01

Brief the business

Who visits, what is the one action that matters — book, buy, call, enrol — and what gets in their way today?

02

Design the flow

Layout, type and colour chosen for that audience first; the hero answers “what is this?” in five seconds.

03

Build it interactive

The key action is never a picture. Menus filter, prices calculate, carts count — in vanilla JavaScript.

04

Refine for everyone

Checked at phone width, with a keyboard, and in dark mode before it earns a place in the set.
